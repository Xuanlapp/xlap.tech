# Neutral dark product cards

- Goal: remove legacy navy card surfaces so product workspaces match the neutral charcoal dark-mode palette.
- Scope: shared dark-mode compatibility rules for Glass cards and status toolbar.
- Preserve: blue/cyan/indigo action colors and all card content/behavior.
- Acceptance: cards use charcoal panel #181b21, inner header/toolbar uses muted charcoal #22262e, and action colors remain semantic accents.
